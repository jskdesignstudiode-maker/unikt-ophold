Glamping in Denmark

Tips

Tips for your stay

A few simple tips to make your night in a tent, cabin or treehouse even more enjoyable.

Glamping in winter

There is something special about snuggling under the duvet while it is cold outside. Before you book, check for a wood stove or heating, good insulation and a private bathroom.

Travelling with a dog

Dogs love being outdoors too. Ask the host whether dogs are welcome, whether there is a fence and room to run. Some hosts charge an extra cleaning fee.

The best time to go

July is often one of the busiest and most expensive months. May, June and September often bring lower prices, mild weather and a quieter atmosphere.

Packing for glamping

Bring warm clothes, even in summer. Evenings by the coast can be cool. Ask whether bed linen and towels are included, and you can travel light.

Book early

The most sought-after places are often booked months ahead in the summer holidays. Flexible dates give you more to choose from.

Check distance and parking

Many places are wonderfully secluded, and that is the point. Ask about parking and the distance to the nearest shop, so you know what to bring.

Questions and answers

Frequently asked questions

What is glamping?

Glamping is camping with comfort. You get a real bed, often a private bathroom and heating, and at some places a sauna or spa. You get nature without the hassle.

When is the best time for glamping in Denmark?

May to September is the most pleasant. Many places are open all year and have heating, so autumn and winter can be cosy too. Look for a wood stove or other heating.

Can I bring my dog?

It depends on the place. Use the Dogs welcome filter to see only the stays where dogs are welcome.

How do I book?

You click through to the host’s own page and book directly with them. You can check prices and availability there.

Does it cost anything to use Unikt Ophold?

No. You only pay the host for your stay. We do not charge guests any fees.

What does Ad mean?

Ad means a host has paid for extra visibility. Paid placements are always marked.
