Tips

Tips for your stay

Small tips from us, so your night in a tent, cabin or treehouse becomes even better.

Glamping in winter

There is something special about lying warm under a window while it is cold outside. Before you book, check for a wood stove or heating, good insulation and a private bathroom.

Travelling with a dog

Dogs love being outdoors too. Ask the host whether dogs are welcome, whether there is a fence and room to run. Some places charge a cleaning fee.

The best time to go

July is the busiest and most expensive month. May, June and September often bring lower prices, mild air and more calm around you.

Packing for glamping

Bring warm clothes, even in summer. Evenings by the coast can be cool. Ask whether bed linen and towels are included, and you can travel light.

Book early

The most sought-after places are often booked months ahead in the summer holidays. Flexible dates give you more to choose from.

Check distance and parking

Many places lie beautifully remote, and that is the point. Ask about parking and the distance to the nearest shop, so you know what to bring.

Questions and answers

Frequently asked questions

What is glamping?

Glamping is camping with comfort. You get a real bed, often your own bathroom and warmth, and at some places a sauna or spa. You get nature without the hassle.

When is the best time for glamping in Denmark?

May to September is the most pleasant. Many places are open all year and warm, so autumn and winter can be cosy too. Look for a wood stove.

Can I bring my dog?

It depends on the place. Use the Dogs welcome filter to see only the stays where dogs are welcome.

Questions and answers

What is the difference between glamping and camping?

With glamping the tent or cabin and the bed are usually already in place. You bring clothes and food, and the rest is ready.

When can you go glamping in Denmark?

Many places are open from spring to autumn. Some have heating and stay open longer, so always check the individual stay.
